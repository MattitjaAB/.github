# Mattitja AB

**Small in size, big in impact.**
We build smart websites and digital solutions that make a real difference for businesses and their users. With the right technology in the right place, we deliver fast, stable and user friendly systems that grow alongside your company.

With more than ten years of experience and over a hundred completed projects, we have helped companies overcome everything from small technical hurdles to complex business challenges.

When we join a project, we often see opportunities for improvement beyond the initial scope. These are documented as issues in the project’s GitHub repository and solved continuously so our clients can focus on their business.

## Our stack

We work primarily with:
- **Laravel** as our framework for applications
- **Statamic** as a flexible and powerful CMS
- **Livewire** for building dynamic interfaces within Laravel
- **Tailwind CSS** for modern and efficient styling
- **Pest** for clean and delightful testing
